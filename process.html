From Leaf To Bottle

Every bottle of Craft Kombucha takes a slow 21-day journey. No shortcuts, no pasteurization, no compromises — just real tea, real fruit, and time.

What Is Kombucha?

Fermented Tea, Bubbly & Alive

Kombucha is a fermented tea drink made by adding a live culture (called a SCOBY) to sweetened tea. Over time, the culture eats the sugar and turns it into a tangy, lightly fizzy, probiotic-rich beverage.

We brew ours the slow way — 21 days from start to bottle. No pasteurization, no artificial flavors, no shortcuts. Just real ingredients and a live culture doing its job.

Live Culture · Real Brewing
The Brewing Process

Our 5-Step Journey

From a kettle of tea to a bottled pour — here's exactly how every bottle of Craft Kombucha is made.

Step 01 · Day 01

Brew The Tea

Organic green and black tea leaves are steeped in filtered water, then sweetened with raw cane sugar to feed the live culture.

01
02
Step 02 · Day 02–09

First Fermentation

The sweet tea meets our live SCOBY culture. Over seven slow days, it ferments into a tangy, probiotic-rich kombucha.

Step 03 · Day 10–17

Flavor & Second Ferment

Real fruit, herbs, and spices are added. A second ferment builds natural carbonation and layers in the flavor of each family — Citrus, Berry, or Spice.

03
04
Step 04 · Day 18–21

Bottle & Chill

Bottled cold, never pasteurized. Live cultures preserved, natural fizz locked in, ready for the taproom or your fridge.

Step 05 · Day 21+

Ready To Pour

From the taproom taps to your doorstep — every bottle is hand-checked for fizz, clarity, and flavor before it goes out. Then it's yours to enjoy.

Our Ingredients

Four Things. Nothing Else.

We keep our ingredient list short on purpose — every single one earns its place in the bottle.

Organic Tea

Premium green and black tea leaves — the base of every brew. Sourced from certified organic growers.

Live SCOBY

Our house culture — a living symbiotic colony that ferments the tea and gives every bottle its probiotics.

Real Fruit

Whole fruit — berries, citrus, stone fruit — pressed fresh for each batch. Never concentrates, never syrups.

Herbs & Spices

Fresh ginger, turmeric, cardamom, hibiscus, and mint — for warmth, color, and complexity.

Live & Raw

Why We Never Pasteurize

Most supermarket kombucha is pasteurized — heat-treated to extend shelf life. That kills the live cultures along with the fizz. We refuse to do it.

Live Probiotics Billions of live cultures in every bottle — good for your gut, alive in every sip.
Natural Fizz Carbonation from fermentation itself — light, lively, and never artificially forced.
Real Flavor No concentrates or extracts. Just fruit, herbs, and slow fermentation doing the work.
