The Experience

More Than Just A Drink

Our taproom is a place to slow down, sip something real, and learn a little about fermentation along the way.

Guided Tasting Flights

Choose any four taps and our team walks you through each pour — the ingredients, the fermentation, the story behind it. Perfect for first-timers.

Community Brew Nights

Monthly brewing workshops where you learn to make your own kombucha at home. Small groups, hands-on, and everyone leaves with a starter jar.

Private Events

Host your birthday, team meetup, or tasting party in the taproom. We handle the flights, the setup, and the storytelling. You bring the guests.
