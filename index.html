Why Choose Craft Kombucha

Four Reasons To Sip With Us

We keep it simple: real ingredients, slow fermentation, and honest brewing — every single batch.

Small-Batch Brewed

Every batch is fermented in-house in small glass vessels — never mass-produced, never rushed.

Real Ingredients

Whole fruit, fresh herbs, and raw cane sugar. No concentrates, no artificial flavors, ever.

Live & Raw

Unpasteurized and full of live cultures — good for your gut and full of natural fizz.

Taproom Fresh
