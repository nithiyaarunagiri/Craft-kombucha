Our Story

It Started With One Jar In A Tiny Kitchen

Back in 2018, our founder brewed the very first batch of kombucha on a kitchen countertop — one glass jar, one SCOBY, and a wild idea that real kombucha could taste incredible without any shortcuts.

Friends asked for bottles. Neighbors asked for growlers. Eventually the entire block wanted a flavor. So we rented a small space, bought more jars, and turned a hobby into a fully-fledged taproom and bottling company.

Today we brew 24 flavors across three families, but every bottle still starts the same way: real tea, real fruit, and 21 slow days of fermentation.

Priya Nair Founder & Head Brewer
What Drives Us

Our Mission & Vision

Two simple ideas keep every batch honest — and every bottle worth drinking.

Our Mission

To brew the most honest, gut-loving kombucha on the market — with real ingredients, live cultures, and zero shortcuts. Every bottle should make you feel as good as it tastes, from the first sip to the last.

Our Vision

To build a kombucha culture where people gather, sip slowly, and celebrate what real fermentation can do — for their tastebuds, their guts, and their communities. Five taprooms by 2028, zero waste along the way.

Our Journey

How We Grew Up

From a single kitchen jar to a full taproom and bottling company — here are the milestones that shaped Craft Kombucha.

2018

First Batch Brewed

One jar, one SCOBY, one curious founder. The first kombucha fermented on a kitchen counter in Manchester.

Beginning
2019

Farmers Market Debut

Started selling bottles at local weekend markets — sold out three weeks in a row and knew we were onto something.

First Sales
2021

Taproom Opened

Opened our first taproom on Ferment Lane with 6 rotating taps. Friday flight nights became a local ritual.

Milestone
2023

Bottling Line Launched

Expanded to full bottle production and launched the Kombucha Club — a monthly subscription shipping nationwide.

Expansion
2024

24 Flavors Milestone

Hit our 24th flavor release with the seasonal Brewing Calendar. Three families, one obsession with fermentation.

Achievement
Today

Still Brewing Fresh

Thousands of bottles poured, hundreds of flights served. The next batch is already fermenting in the taproom.
